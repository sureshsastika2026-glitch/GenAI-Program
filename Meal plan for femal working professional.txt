# T – TASK

The plan must include:

- Breakfast
- Lunch
- Evening snack
- Dinner

For each meal, provide:

1. A specific South Indian dish name.
2. Appropriate portion size.
3. A short preparation suggestion when necessary.

Ensure the meals are nutritious, varied, easy to prepare and suitable for a busy work schedule.
